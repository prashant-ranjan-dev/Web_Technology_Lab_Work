About the Department

The Department of Computer Science brings together foundational knowledge, practical experience and a culture of curiosity.

Our teaching combines theory with programming, project work and collaborative problem-solving. Students learn to understand systems, question assumptions and design useful solutions for a changing world.

Faculty and learners work across artificial intelligence, data, software engineering, cybersecurity and human-centered computing. Through workshops, seminars, projects and peer learning, the department prepares its community for higher study, research and meaningful careers.
